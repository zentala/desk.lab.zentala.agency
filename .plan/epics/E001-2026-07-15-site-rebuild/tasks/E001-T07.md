# E001-T07: Strip EXIF/GPS + downscale the raw phone JPEGs

**Wave 2** · independent.

## Goal
Remove home GPS coordinates from the photos and shrink them for the web.

## Acceptance
- Explicit `exiftool -gps:all= -all=` (or equivalent) on the 6 raw `20230628_*.jpg` (~19 MB of 20 MB).
- Downscaled to display size; per-image budget ~500 KB. **Verify with `exiftool` after** — do not assume the framework strips it.
- The check runs over **every** tracked image path, not one directory.

## Where the files are (verified 2026-07-29)
`legacy/images/` and `astro/public/images/` each hold the same 11 files, byte-identical,
19.7 MB per copy. [T04](E001-T04.md) collapses this to one copy — run T07 **after** T04 or the
strip has to be done twice and the CI gate has to cover both paths.
