# E003-T06: Source electronics review

## Objective

Find a qualified electronics professional, firm or appropriately experienced
community reviewer and provide the self-contained review pack.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Reviewer candidates are assessed for relevant PCB and low-voltage USB/I2C experience.
